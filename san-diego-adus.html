San Diego ADUs

Last verified against official source pages: June 26, 2026 (Pacific Time).

What This Page Covers

This page is designed as a high-utility reference for homeowners, designers, and builders who need San Diego ADU and JADU rules in one place. It prioritizes official City and State source links so you can confirm details quickly before submittal.

Current ADU And JADU Rule Snapshot

  • ADU: attached or detached unit on a lot with a proposed or existing primary residence, with independent living facilities.
  • JADU: up to 500 square feet, within a single-family residence, with a kitchen or efficiency kitchen.
  • City indicates a building permit is required to create an ADU or JADU.
  • City comparison table lists ADU size range at 150 to 1,200 square feet and JADU size range at 150 to 500 square feet.
  • City notes parking is generally not required for ADUs except in the Beach Impact Area; JADU parking is listed as not required.

Setbacks, Height, And Encroachments

  • City guidance indicates no side/rear setback is necessary for certain ADUs under 16 feet outside Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones.
  • A minimum 4-foot side/rear setback applies when ADU/JADU height exceeds 16 feet adjacent to residential premises, and in Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones.
  • New detached ADU structures must still comply with the zone front setback; side/rear flexibility may apply per code.
  • City states ADUs may encroach into side/rear setbacks, but certain features (for example exterior staircases and roof decks) cannot encroach.

Bonus ADU, Accessible Bonus, And Condominiums

  • City ADU Bonus Program: in Sustainable Development Areas, one additional non-deed restricted bonus ADU may be allowed for each qualifying deed-restricted ADU, with a stated 15-year affordability term.
  • Outside Sustainable Development Areas, City states the maximum number of bonus ADUs is one when qualifying deed-restricted ADUs are proposed.
  • Accessible Bonus ADU pathway may apply when two affordable ADUs are provided and accessibility standards are met.
  • City indicates ADU condominiums can be separately sold in certain cases, while ADUs built through the ADU Home Density Bonus program are not eligible for separate sale.

Permit Path And Plan Review

  1. 1) Pre-check zoning and overlays

    Confirm zoning, Coastal Overlay status, and Sustainable Development Area status in City tools before design decisions.

  2. 2) Select custom or preapproved plans

    The City accepts certain regional preapproved plans and notes a 30-day review target for applications using preapproved plans, subject to code compliance.

  3. 3) Submit building permit application

    Apply through Development Services and track review milestones through City systems.

  4. 4) Final inspections and closeout

    Complete inspections and finalize required documents, including any JADU agreement or subdivision-related filings if applicable.
