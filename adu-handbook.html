City of San Diego vs. unincorporated County: what changes

Last reviewed October 2, 2026

The handbook below is the County of San Diego April 2024 guide for unincorporated areas. City lots follow Information Bulletin 400 (January 2026).

The City zoning page says every City property has a zoning designation, and Bulletin 400 says to find it in the Zoning and Parcel Information Portal. For an unincorporated parcel, start with the County ADU page, the Initial Study Research Report, or the Property Summary Report (you need the assessor's parcel number).

Topic City of San Diego County handbook
Size Attached or detached ADU: 150 to 1,200 square feet. A conversion of an existing accessory structure, or part of the primary home, is not capped at 1,200. JADU: 150 to 500 square feet. Detached max 1,200 square feet. Attached: 50 percent of the house or 1,200, whichever is less. The handbook lists attached exceptions of 850, 1,000, and 1,200 square feet by bedroom count. JADU max 500.
How many One primary home, one attached or detached ADU, one converted ADU, and one JADU. Including bonus units, the cap is 4 on lots of 8,000 square feet or less, 5 from 8,001 to 10,000, and 6 above that. One ADU and one JADU on a single-family lot. Two detached ADUs at most on a multifamily lot.
Setbacks Zone front setback. Street side is that setback or 4 feet, whichever is less. Side and rear may meet the lot line unless the building code requires more. Over 16 feet beside a home: 4 feet or the zone minimum, whichever is less. Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone: at least 4 feet. One ADU of 800 square feet or less may enter the front setback. 4-foot side and rear setbacks, and the zone front setback. A detached ADU may use the front setback only if the lot precludes building outside it. Conversions keep existing setbacks.
Parking No ADU parking outside the Coastal Overlay Zone. Inside it, one space in the Beach Impact Area and outside a transit priority area, unless an exception applies. No JADU parking. One space for a new ADU unless it is within one-half mile of public transit or in an architecturally and historically significant historic district. No parking for a conversion or a JADU.
Fees Permit fees are in Bulletin 501. The inclusionary in-lieu fee is in Bulletin 532. Pay the required fees. The trial waiver ended January 9, 2024.
Review A building permit is required. Apply online under Building Construction. A coastal unit that is not fully inside the existing home, or that adds habitable area or converts non-habitable space, also needs a Coastal Development Permit. Planning and Development Services, 5510 Overland Ave, San Diego, CA 92123. Planning, engineering, and fire districts typically review.

Bulletin 501 (August 2026) says two or more new units must provide affordable housing or pay an in-lieu fee. Bulletin 532 counts 10 new units outside the Coastal Overlay Zone, five inside it, and two for condominium conversions. Bulletin 400 counts 10 total units, or five in that overlay, for an ADU project. Those counts differ. If a County project started before January 9, 2024, plan check was not charged, but a permit not issued by that date still owes issuance fees.

The County ADU page links zoning sheet PDS-611, revised September 2, 2026. On a single-family lot that sheet allows one new detached ADU, a conversion of permitted existing space, and one JADU. The April 2024 handbook still says one ADU and one JADU, and it points readers to the County's ADU and JADU Regulations handout for the updated list. Some Bulletin 400 rules are not yet effective in the Coastal Overlay Zone.
