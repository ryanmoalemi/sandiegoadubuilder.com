Our 4-step process

  1. Site + Feasibility

    We assess zoning, lot constraints, setbacks, and utility access so you know what is possible from day one.

  2. Design + Engineering

    Plans are developed around your goals, then coordinated with structural and MEP engineering.

  3. Permits + Approvals

    Our team handles submittals and city comments to keep your project moving without paperwork headaches.

  4. Construction + Handover

    Dedicated project management, quality control, and final walkthrough before keys are delivered.

San Diego ADU Builder FAQs

How much does it cost to build an ADU in San Diego?

Cost depends on size, type, site access, and utility work. Use our estimator for a planning range, then request a tailored feasibility review.

How long does an ADU project take in San Diego?

A typical timeline is design and permits first, then construction. Most projects are completed in phases over several months depending on city review and complexity.

What types of ADUs do your San Diego ADU builders handle?

We build detached ADUs, garage conversions, and attached ADUs for rental income, multigenerational living, and flexible home-office use.
