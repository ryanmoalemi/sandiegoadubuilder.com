Common questions

How much does an ADU cost in San Diego?

Cost depends on size, type, site access, and utility work. Use the calculator for a planning range, then read the ADU cost guide and the feasibility guide.

How long does an ADU project take in San Diego?

Design and permits come first, then construction. The permitting guide explains why review time depends on the jurisdiction and the completeness of the plans.

Which ADU types does this guide cover?

The guides cover detached ADUs, garage conversions, attached ADUs, and JADUs.

How much does an ADU permit cost in San Diego?

The City of San Diego says the permit fee depends on the project and points to Information Bulletin 501 for the schedule. See the permit fee section.

How do City and County ADU requirements differ?

A lot in the City of San Diego is reviewed by City Development Services (city ADU page). See San Diego ADUs.

A lot in unincorporated San Diego County is reviewed by County Planning and Development Services (county ADU page). See the ADU Handbook.
